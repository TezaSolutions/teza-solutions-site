Payroll

45 min → 3 min

Timesheets read, hours computed, rates applied, document sent to the accountant. Every two weeks, thirteen employees. The system caught a calculation error before it went out.

What we don't do

A system that decides for you ends up costing you. Here is where we always stop.

Never

We don't touch your money. A transfer, an order, a payment: the system prepares, you approve. No exceptions.

Never

We don't write to anyone on your behalf without approval. Client email, public reply, post: it's drafted, you send.

Never

We don't replace your accountant or your team. We remove the repetitive work around them, not the judgment.

Never

We don't sell you a system we don't monitor. What runs unwatched breaks silently — we refuse to ship that.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most

How much does it cost? How is pricing determined?
Pricing depends on the solution you need. Since every project is custom-built, we set pricing after an initial call that lets us fully understand your needs and the scope of the work.
Do I have to change my current tools?
Our goal is not to disrupt your habits: we work with the tools you already use. A change is sometimes necessary, but we always explain it clearly and keep it to a strict minimum.
Is my data secure?
The security of your data is an absolute priority. We adapt to each company's needs, including, for example, hosting your databases in Canada if you wish.
Why not just do it myself with AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Zapier)?
You can, and for a simple, one-off need, that might be enough. But a system that needs to hold up over time — that handles errors, that evolves with your business, that you don't have to watch yourself — is a different job than making a script run once. That's exactly why we exist: we build what stays solid even when you're not looking anymore.
